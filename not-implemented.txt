
These are still missing or only partially implemented:

1. Live provider activation: Zoom, Google Calendar, Stripe and DocuSign adapters exist, but real accounts and transactions/signatures haven’t been verified.
2. Production rollout: Production migration, deployment and live acceptance testing remain pending.
3. Itemized invoices: Quantity, unit price, line items, subtotal, discount and tax calculations.
4. Gateway refund handling: Automatic refunds, disputes, chargebacks and settlement reconciliation.
5. Client messages: An approved client-visible messages/updates feed.
6. Profile fields: Dedicated address fields and separate consultant assignment per application.
7. Task details: Cancelled status and dedicated task/meeting notes.
8. Overdue actions: Complete/reschedule directly from the popup, plus a dedicated overdue timeline event.
9. Notifications: Some suggested types, including upcoming Zoom meetings and overdue invoices, lack dedicated notifications.
10. Complete immutable timeline: Major events exist, but general activity history isn’t universally append-only.
11. Application automation: Automatic movement after payment and a separate client-friendly status mapping.
12. Commercial details: Itemized proposal PDFs and synchronization of the CRM contract-expiry date with DocuSign’s envelope expiry.

The portal already has restricted authentication through private sign-in links; permanent username/password client accounts aren’t implemented.
