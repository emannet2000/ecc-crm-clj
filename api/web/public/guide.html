How to use: a typical working day

  1. Open Today. Review overdue tasks, application deadlines and documents waiting for review. Open a record to act on it. Lists show a few items; use the full screens for everything.
  2. Find the person first. Search Leads or Students before adding anyone. A lead is an enquiry; a student is a person receiving a service. When a lead proceeds, use the lead’s conversion action to retain their history.
  3. Check the student summary. Review the current application, next task, missing requirements and assigned officer together. Add a task with a due date and an owner when follow-up is needed.
  4. Update the application. Record the destination, service, school, program, intake, admission status and next deadline. Update milestones as events happen.
  5. Review requirements. Request missing documents. After an upload passes scanning, check its contents and update its review status. Scan approval alone does not mean a requirement is accepted.
  6. Record the outcome. Add a note or log a conversation, then set the next task. Record money received through Payments and accounting.

User manual

Current date, time and deadlines

The clock panel shows the current date, time with seconds, business timezone and synchronization status on every staff workspace page. It updates every second and synchronizes with the server each minute. If it says Time not synchronized, check your connection before relying on the displayed time for a critical deadline.

Today counts refresh at business midnight. Task and application deadlines currently use a date, so they become overdue after that date ends in the business timezone. Record exact school cutoff times in the application notes and verify the school’s timezone; the date field alone does not represent a 3 PM cutoff.

Navigation and access

Desktop navigation shows the main screens and More tools. On a phone, use the bottom tabs and More. Help opens this guide in a separate tab.

Administrators can restrict access further. Assigned-record restrictions and permissions for documents, exports, email, the portal and finance may limit what you can do. Ask the administrator if an action is unavailable; sharing passwords is not a workaround.

Add or update a student

  1. Search Students for the person. Check Leads as well if they originally made an enquiry.
  2. Use the add action, enter their name and contact details, and assign an officer where available.
  3. Save. If a duplicate warning appears, review the linked records first. Shared family phone numbers can be valid; save as a separate record only after checking.
  4. Open the student page to update details and manage related applications, requirements and tasks.

Keep contact details current. A duplicate warning is advisory and can only show records your account is allowed to access.

Applications, school details and milestones

Use Applications to track each service separately. Record school and program names, offer and enrollment status, destination, intake and the next deadline. A student can have several applications; open the application list to see all of them.

Use More tools → Milestones for offer, deposit, visa submission, visa decision and arrival dates. Choose an application, enter a planned date and update the status when it changes. Completed milestones require a completion date. Recording a deposit milestone does not record a payment or change the financial balance.

Requirements and documents

An administrator can maintain Checklist templates. Apply a suitable template to an application, then verify the requirements against the actual school and destination. Starter checklists are editable examples, not an official or complete requirements list.

Upload the correct document and wait for malware scanning. Then review its contents and mark its requirement appropriately. Track expiry dates and replace expired evidence. If a scan fails, ask an administrator to investigate or retry; do not treat a pending or failed scan as accepted.

Student photos

Open a student’s photo card and choose one PNG or JPEG, up to 2 MB and no larger than 4096 × 4096 pixels. The photo becomes visible after malware scanning passes. If someone else replaced it, refresh before uploading again.

Tasks and conversations

Give tasks a clear action, due date, priority and responsible staff member. Mark finished tasks Done. Notes and history retain the context of earlier work.

Use Calls and messages to record an interaction’s channel, direction, outcome, date and next action. WhatsApp and SMS links open your usual messaging app; you review and send there. Save the conversation log separately. Logging a conversation does not send a message.

Send email uses the CRM’s email queue and requires administrator configuration. A queued message is not proof of delivery. Automatic WhatsApp or SMS sending is not configured by the manual links or logs.

Payments and corrections

Use Payments and accounting to record money received against the correct student or invoice, currency, date, method and reference. Check the amount before saving and review the payment history afterward.

For an incorrect posted entry, use the supported reversal/correction workflow with a reason and create the correct entry. Avoid entering another payment to hide a mistake. Closed accounting periods restrict changes; an administrator must review corrections affecting them.

Student portal and account security

Authorized staff can manage Student portal invitations. Confirm the recipient before sharing access. Students should use their own portal access rather than a staff account.

Use Account security to manage available security settings. Log out on shared devices. If the app reports that a record changed, compare the latest record with your draft before saving again.

Frequently asked questions

Can Flor work without WhatsApp or SMS integration?

Yes. Student records, applications, tasks, documents and payments do not depend on automatic messaging. Use a phone or WhatsApp directly and record the conversation afterward.

Where do I enable automatic WhatsApp or SMS?

The manual links and conversation log do not include an automatic provider setup screen. Automatic sending would require an implemented provider connection, an account, credentials and any provider charges.

Why is my photo or document not visible yet?

Uploads stay quarantined until malware scanning passes. If the scanner is unavailable or reports an error, ask the administrator to check the scanner service. A repeated upload may not resolve a service problem.

Why can’t I find a student or use an action?

Check spelling and filters, then ask the administrator about your role, assigned records and permissions. The record may also be archived or still listed as a lead.

What should I do about a duplicate warning?

Open the suggested records and compare names and contact details. Update the existing record when it is the same person. If two people intentionally share contact details, confirm that they are separate people before continuing.

Does Completed on a deposit milestone count as payment?

No. Record the money through Payments and accounting. A milestone is used to track an application event.

Why did saving stop with a record-changed message?

Another action updated the record after you opened it. Refresh or compare the current version, retain the changes you still need, and save against the latest version. Check payment history before retrying a financial submission.

Is a starter checklist enough for every application?

No. Verify the current requirements for the student’s school, destination and circumstances, then tailor the checklist.

What should I do if an upload or save fails?

Keep your entered text, check your connection and read the error. Sign in again if asked. Check whether the change already appears before retrying, particularly for payments. Give the administrator the time, screen and error message without sharing passwords.

Before relying on the app for daily operations, the administrator should verify the deployed version, database connection, backups, scanning and required delivery services. This guide describes the app’s workflows; it does not confirm that a particular deployment is configured.
